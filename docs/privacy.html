bu66le Privacy Policy

Effective: June 22, 2026

bu66le is a minimalist bubble level (spirit level) for Android. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle. The short version: bu66le collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Data we collect

None. bu66le does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or crash-reporting services.

Device sensors

bu66le reads your device's accelerometer to measure tilt and position the bubble. These readings are used only on your device, in real time, to draw the level. They are never recorded to permanent storage, shared, or sent off the device. Any angles you "record" inside the app are kept only in memory for the current session and are gone when the app closes.

Permissions

bu66le requests no special or sensitive permissions. The accelerometer is a motion sensor that does not require a runtime permission, and the app needs no internet access to function.

Network & third parties

bu66le does not connect to the internet, contains no advertising, and embeds no third-party SDKs or tracking libraries. There is no data to be sold or shared, and none is.

Children

bu66le is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
