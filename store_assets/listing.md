## Full description (max 4000 chars)
bu66le is a minimalist bubble level (spirit level) for your phone. Lay your
device on any surface and a glowing bubble drifts toward the raised side — it
turns green the moment you're level. No clutter, no ads, no sign-ups.

FEATURES

• 2D bubble level — a circular vial with crosshair and target ring. The bubble
  drifts toward the high corner just like a real spirit level, so you can see
  tilt in both directions at once.

• Single-axis tracks — separate horizontal (X) and vertical (Y) tracks for
  dialing in one axis at a time, perfect for hanging shelves, frames and TVs.

• Live numeric readout — X and Y tilt shown in degrees, turning green when you
  are within ±1° of level.

• Record & review — snapshot the current angles to a running list (newest
  first) and clear them when you're done.

HOW IT WORKS

bu66le reads your device's accelerometer and smooths the signal to damp noise,
then derives tilt angles from the gravity vector. Everything runs on-device in
real time.

PRIVACY

bu66le collects no personal data, has no ads, and requires no special
permissions or internet connection. The accelerometer reading never leaves your
phone.

TIPS

• For best accuracy, place the phone on a flat, solid surface.
• A quick calibration against a surface you know is level helps account for any
  hardware offset in your device.

bu66le is free and open source. Source code and issues:
https://github.com/uzbit/bu66le
